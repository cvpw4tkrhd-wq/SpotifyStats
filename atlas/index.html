Reading your export

How to get your data

  1. Open spotify.com/account/privacy and scroll to "Download your data".
  2. Choose Extended streaming history (all years, the most complete). "Account data" also works but only covers the last year.
  3. Confirm by email. Spotify can take up to 30 days to prepare it.
  4. Download the zip from the email and drop it here without opening it.

Your privacy

Your file is read in this browser and the results are kept in this browser. Names of artists and albums are looked up at Last.fm, Deezer and MusicBrainz to add genres, photos, covers, release years and countries. Nothing else leaves your device, and you can delete it all from the menu at any time.

Listening Atlas is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify.

Highlights

    About and privacy

    Listening Atlas turns the data export you can request from Spotify into charts and stories about your listening. It is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify.

    • Your export is read inside this browser. It is never uploaded to a server, and there are no accounts.
    • Results are stored in this browser only. Clearing site data or using "Delete my data" removes them.
    • To add genres, photos, covers, release years and artist countries, the names of your artists and albums are sent to Last.fm, Deezer and MusicBrainz. Each service has its own privacy policy.
    • Only music listening is used. Podcasts and private-session listening are left out.
